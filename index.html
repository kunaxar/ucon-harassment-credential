What changes for UCON

Same quiz, same link, same class. Three fields added. No dev work and no list from UCON.

1 · Add three fields to the quiz

  • Name
  • Email
  • Confirm email — the learner types the email twice, so typos get caught.
Works the same in Zoho Survey and in Monday forms.

2 · BuilderFAX does the rest

  • Creates the BuilderFAX account on the email the learner entered.
  • Issues the credential under the name they entered.
  • Emails it to them.
How the quiz tells BuilderFAX about a submission (webhook) is a separate conversation.

3 · Known risk

The email is not verified. Someone could enter a fake email on purpose. The confirm-email field only catches honest typos. We are accepting this for launch.
