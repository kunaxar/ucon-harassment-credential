Under SB 1343, all California companies with 5 or more employees must provide sexual harassment training to all employees. Non-supervisory employees must receive at least one hour of sexual harassment training, and supervisors must receive at least two hours of sexual harassment training.

This training must be renewed every two years and provided to new hires within six months of their hire date or promotion to a supervisory position.

This course meets the state-mandated requirements. The first hour is designed for all employees, and the second hour contains information specifically for supervisors.

What You Will Learn
  • How state and federal laws define “sexual harassment” and how to identify it in the workplace
  • What to do if you experience or witness harassment, and how reporting protects you

What changes for UCON

Same quiz, same link, same class. Four fields and a consent page are added to the quiz. This page describes the proposal; the actual setup depends on UCON's Zoho or Monday plan and on BuilderFAX confirming its side.

1 · Page 1: add four fields to the quiz

  • Name
  • Email
  • Confirm email — the learner types the email twice, so typos get caught.
  • Phone number
The prototype shows both the Zoho Survey and Monday looks. Whether each can be set up this way on UCON's actual plan is still to be checked.

2 · Page 2: consent before anything is created

  • Tells the learner BuilderFAX will create an account from their name, email and phone number, and email them the credential.
  • A required agree box. Without it the learner cannot submit, and no account or credential email follows.
  • Changing any detail, or going back, clears the agreement so they must agree again.
The consent wording is a draft for UCON and BuilderFAX to review. It is not legal advice.

3 · BuilderFAX does the rest (to be confirmed)

  • Only for submissions where consent was given: creates the BuilderFAX account, issues the credential under the name entered, and emails it.
  • Ignores any submission without consent, even if the quiz sends it.
This needs the quiz to send each completed response to BuilderFAX (a webhook or similar), plus BuilderFAX work to create accounts and send the email. That engineering and the plan requirements are still to be confirmed. The prototype only simulates it.

4 · Known risk

The email is not verified. Someone could enter a fake email on purpose. The confirm-email field only catches honest typos.
